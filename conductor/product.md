# Product Guide - Official Website for the State Agency for Tourism Development of Ukraine (DART)

## Vision
To provide a modern, accessible, and high-performance official digital presence for Ukraine's state tourism authority, facilitating communication between the government, citizens, and the tourism industry.

## Target Audience
- **Domestic & International Tourists:** Seeking official information, travel stories, and safety updates.
- **Tourism Business Owners:** Tour operators and hotel owners requiring licensing, categorization, and regulatory information.
- **Government & Partners:** Accessing official reports, statistics, and organizational updates.

## Core Objectives
- **Official Information Hub:** Centralized platform for all news, announcements, and state policies in the tourism sector.
- **Regulatory Transparency:** Provide clear access to licensing registries, hotel categorization standards, and official documents.
- **Modernized UX:** Seamless migration from legacy Webflow to a high-performance Next.js and Payload CMS stack.

## Key Features & Capabilities
- **Localization (UK/EN):** Full multi-language support with URL-based routing.
- **Dynamic Content Management:** Powerful administration of news, team members, departments, and vacancies via Payload CMS.
- **Hybrid Content Architecture:** Optimal mix of fast static pages and flexible dynamic components.
- **Accessibility:** Targeted WCAG AA compliance to ensure inclusivity for all users.

## Strategic Roadmap (Initial)
1. **Infrastructure:** Next.js + Payload CMS + PostgreSQL foundation.
2. **Content Migration:** Transitioning historical data from Webflow CSVs.
3. **Core Modules:** Implementing news, team, and regulatory databases.
4. **Enhanced UI:** High-fidelity header/footer and advanced accessibility features.
