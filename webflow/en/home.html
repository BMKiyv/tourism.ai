The State Agency for Tourism Development of Ukraine

SATD is the central executive authority responsible for implementing the state policy in the field of tourism and resorts in Ukraine. We are dedicated to promoting Ukraine as a global tourism brand, fostering domestic tourism development, and enhancing the quality and competitiveness of the national tourism product. Our efforts aim to attract a greater number of tourists to explore the diverse and alluring attractions of Ukraine.

1

Mission

The Agency is dedicated to fostering the growth of regional tourism potential and conducting impactful marketing campaigns to promote the country as an attractive tourist destination, both domestically and internationally.

2

Vision

To cultivate a competitive, sustainable, and responsible tourism industry that contributes to the creation of new employment opportunities, ensures accessibility of tourist products for all categories of travelers, and guarantees the sustainable development of communities.

3

Goals

Through collaboration with a diverse network of partners, we seek to enhance Ukraine's reputation among both locals and international visitors as a top leisure destination. In addition, we are committed to raising the tourism sector's contribution to the country's GDP.

Our projects

The State Agency for Tourism Development of Ukraine actively implements projects aimed at strengthening and enriching the country's tourism industry. Our project activities focus on creating innovative tourist routes, developing infrastructure, promoting regional growth, and supporting local initiatives. We actively collaborate with local communities, businesses, and non-governmental organizations to ensure the sustainable development of tourism. Our projects contribute to the preservation of cultural heritage, create new job opportunities, and promote awareness of the unique tourist destinations that Ukraine provides.

#Travelaroundukraine

The project is aimed at promoting and developing domestic tourism, encouraging Ukrainians to explore their own country and discover new exciting locations.

UkraineNOW

Our project presents Ukraine as an attractive tourist destination to a global audience, contributing to the enhancement of its investment appeal.

Commemoration routes

The project introduces a new tourism paradigm for Ukraine, centering on sites that honor the memory of those who sacrificed their lives for Ukrainian independence. It outlines a strategic vision aimed at establishing destinations and routes that commemorate the sacrifices made by the Ukrainian people in the pursuit of freedom, peace, and security.
